🎬 TecnicoAV - Professional Audiovisual Technology

TécnicoAV is the audiovisual technical division of DPCproducciones, focused on audio video and lighting systems, and audiovisual training.

TécnicoAV offers these professional audiovisual tutorials and technical documentation for students of audio and video, multimedia event workers, and audiovisual production staff.
